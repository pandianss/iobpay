Questions

What merchants usually ask

Do we need our own website to use IOB Pay?

No. Under the standalone option the bank hosts the collection page and manages the data — you simply direct payers to it. A website and a technical team are needed only if you want the API integration.

What does it cost to set up?

Nothing. No integration charges and no annual maintenance fees are collected from merchants integrated with IOB Pay. You pay only the per-transaction commercial for the mode the payer used.

When does the money reach us?

On T+2. Collected funds are settled to the merchant's settlement account as per the settlement cycle — at present on a T+2 working day basis, where T is the transaction day.

Which modes cost us nothing?

UPI and RuPay debit cards carry NIL merchant charge across every category — worth promoting to your payers if you want collection cost near zero.

How do we reconcile?

Transaction reports are available through the Internet Banking Corporate login under the Utility Payment / Receipt section, and are updated in real time.

Who can become an IOB Pay merchant?

Any customer of the bank with KYC and AML compliance in place, registered for the Corporate Internet Banking login facility.

Who supports us after go-live?

A dedicated helpdesk on 1800 425 4445 / 1800 890 4445 and iobpay.support@iob.in, plus your branch relationship manager.
